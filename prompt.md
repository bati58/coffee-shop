Website Build Prompt — Buna Bet (Full-Service Ethiopian Coffee House & Restaurant)

Design and build a professional, elegant single-page website for Buna Bet, an Ethiopian coffee house and restaurant in Addis Ababa that serves coffee, full meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), and soft drinks.

Brand & Visual Identity (keep consistent throughout)
Warm, moody color palette: deep espresso brown (
#2a1810-ish) backgrounds for hero/dark sections, cream/ivory (
#f5f0e8-ish) for light sections, and a muted gold accent (
#c9a05c-ish) for highlights, prices, and category tags.
Typography: elegant serif for headings (with an italic script accent word, like "story" in the hero), clean sans-serif for body text.
Generous white space, rounded soft cards with subtle shadows, and full-bleed photography.
Tone: warm, authentic, rooted in Ethiopian coffee culture and hospitality — not generic "cafe template" copy.
Site Structure
1. Hero Section

Full-bleed dark hero image (coffee beans or roastery), location + "since" tagline, large serif headline with an italic accent word, subtitle sentence, two CTA buttons ("Explore Menu" and "Reserve a Table" or similar), scroll-down indicator.

2. Our Story

Split layout: large photo (coffee beans, roastery, or kitchen) on one side, story copy on the other — sourcing, tradition, quality. Include 3 short feature callouts with icons (e.g. Ethically Sourced, Fresh Roasted, Made Fresh Daily).

3. Menu — Tabbed, Multi-Category

This is the core expansion. Build a tabbed menu section with these categories (not just "Coffee/Food/Drinks" — break it down):

Coffee (traditional buna, pour-over, espresso drinks, cold brew, spiced coffee)
Breakfast (e.g. ful, firfir, genfo, eggs, pastries — served until a stated time)
Lunch & Dinner (e.g. doro wat, tibs, shiro, vegetarian combo/injera platters, kitfo)
Soft Drinks & Other Beverages (soda, fresh juice, tea, water)

Each item card should show: photo, name, short appetizing description, price in ETB, and an optional tag (Signature / Vegetarian / Spicy / Seasonal / Popular). Use a grid of 3 cards per row, consistent with the existing card style.

4. The Coffee Ceremony

Keep this as a distinct cultural/experience section — numbered steps (Roasting → Grinding → Brewing → Sharing), dark full-bleed background, CTA to book a ceremony.

5. Testimonials

3-column quote cards with guest name and role/description.

6. Visit / Reserve

Two-column: left = location, hours, contact info; right = a reservation form with fields for name, phone, date, time, party size, and a notes field (mention meal type or ceremony request). Include a small map or address block.

7. Footer

Logo + tagline, quick nav links (About, Menu, Ceremony, Visit), social links, copyright line.

Functional Requirements
Sticky nav bar with smooth-scroll links to each section, including a dropdown or grouped link for the multi-category Menu.
Menu tabs must be interactive (clicking Coffee/Breakfast/Lunch & Dinner/Drinks swaps the visible items without reloading the page).
Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop).
Reservation form should include a meal-time or purpose selector (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Coffee & Ceremony) so it fits the expanded offering.
Keep the same premium, editorial feel as the original single-prompt build — this should read as an upgrade, not a redesign.
