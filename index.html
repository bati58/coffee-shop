Our Story

Rooted in tradition, brewed with care

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee. At Buna Bet, we honor that legacy by sourcing beans directly from Yirgacheffe, Sidamo, and Harrar — regions celebrated for their distinct floral and fruity profiles.

Every batch is roasted in small quantities to preserve the delicate character that makes Ethiopian coffee unlike anything else in the world.

  • Ethically Sourced Direct trade with smallholder farmers
  • Fresh Roasted Roasted weekly on-site
  • Traditional Brew Jebena & pour-over methods

The Experience

The Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony

More than a drink — a ritual of community, patience, and hospitality that has been practiced for centuries.

01

Roasting

Green beans are roasted over charcoal until they crack and release their aroma.

02

Grinding

Freshly roasted beans are ground by hand with a mukecha and zenezena.

03

Brewing

Ground coffee simmers in a clay jebena, poured in three rounds — abol, tona, baraka.

04

Sharing

Served with popcorn, roasted barley, and incense — a moment to connect.
